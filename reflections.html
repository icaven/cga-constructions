CGA3d operations (ganja syntax in brackets)
A mirror plane through O with unit normal m is the vector m = m_x e_1 + m_y e_2 + m_z e_3; the mirror plane \Pi = m^{*} is the set of points with X\cdot m = 0, and \Pi_1, \Pi_2, \Pi_3 are the three mirrors.
Reflection of a point: X' = m\, X\, m (a versor sandwich, up to sign; the point is then normalized).
The solid is the orbit of a seed point on the line where two mirrors meet, so the third mirror moves it. The mirrors of A_3, B_3 and H_3 meet at \pi/3, \pi/3; \pi/3, \pi/4; \pi/3, \pi/5 (neighbouring mirrors).
