Points: P(x,y) = n_o + x\,e_1 + y\,e_2 + \tfrac12 (x^2+y^2)\, n_\infty, normalized so -P\cdot n_\infty = 1; then P\cdot Q = -\tfrac12 \lvert p-q\rvert^2.
Circle centred at C with radius |P₁P₂|: \big(C + (P_1\cdot P_2)\, n_\infty\big)^{*}.
\operatorname{split}(T): the two points of a point pair, (T \pm \sqrt{T\cdot T})\,(n_\infty \rfloor T).
\operatorname{pt}(F): the point of a flat point, Y = n_o \rfloor F normalized, P = Y + \tfrac12 (Y\cdot Y)\, n_\infty.
Orange points: centres of the step's circles, s\, n_\infty\, s with s = C^{*}.
Names: C_X(P) circle centred X through P, C_X(\lvert PQ\rvert) with radius |PQ|, K_M Carlyle circle, L_{AB} line, B_{AB} bisector, T point pair, \hat V_k rotor-stepped vertex.
