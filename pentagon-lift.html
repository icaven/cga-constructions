A centre lifted by h gives the circle of radius \sqrt{\rho^2 - h^2}.
\operatorname{split}(T): the two points of a point pair, (T \pm \sqrt{T\cdot T})\,(n_\infty \rfloor T); \operatorname{pt}(F): the point of a flat point.
