GAC and CGA operations (ganja syntax in brackets)
\wedge join (^)   \vee meet (&)   X^{*} dual (!X)   \cdot inner product (|)   \tilde R reverse (~R)
Compass and straightedge work in CGA2d, which sits inside GAC; the conic step works in GAC, \mathbb{R}^{5,3}.
CGA2d point: P(x,y) = n_o + x\,e_1 + y\,e_2 + \tfrac12 (x^2+y^2)\, n_\infty; then P\cdot Q = -\tfrac12 \lvert p-q\rvert^2.
GAC point: \hat P(x,y) = o + x\,e_1 + y\,e_2 + \tfrac12 (x^2+y^2)\, n_\infty + \tfrac12 (x^2-y^2)\, n_{-} + xy\, n_{\times}.
Conic through five points: \hat P_1 \wedge \dots \wedge \hat P_5 \wedge \bar o_{-} \wedge \bar o_{\times}; the meet of two conics is four points.
\iota: CGA2d into GAC, (e_1, e_2, e_3, e_4) \mapsto (e_1, e_2, -e_3, e_6); a circle with dual s becomes \iota(s)\, I. \pi: back to CGA2d, dropping the n_{-}, n_{\times} parts.
Circle centred at C with radius |P₁P₂|: \big(C + (P_1\cdot P_2)\, n_\infty\big)^{*}.
\operatorname{split}(T): the two points of a point pair, (T \pm \sqrt{T\cdot T})\,(n_\infty \rfloor T). \operatorname{split}_4(Q): the four points of a point quadruple, the roots of a quartic.
\operatorname{pt}(F): the point of a flat point, Y = n_o \rfloor F normalized, P = Y + \tfrac12 (Y\cdot Y)\, n_\infty.
Orange points: centres of the step's circles, s\, n_\infty\, s with s = C^{*}. Ringed points: the points of the step's point pairs (violet) and point quadruple (pink).
Names: C_X(P) circle centred X through P, C_X(\lvert PQ\rvert) with radius |PQ|, \mathcal{P} parabola, L_{AB} line, B_{AB} bisector, T point pair, Q_4 point quadruple, \hat V_k rotor-stepped vertex.
